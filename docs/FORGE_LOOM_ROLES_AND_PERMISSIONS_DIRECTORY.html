1 Exact Role Count & Categorization

In FORGE Loom LMS, there are exactly 12 system roles declared in the shared TypeScript domain package (packages/shared-types/src/role.ts) and mirrored in the PostgreSQL Prisma schema enum (enum Role).

College-Scoped 4 Roles

Data access strictly restricted to a single college tenant via collegeId:

  • Student: Course learner & builder.
  • Mentor: Advisory expert & reviewer.
  • Trainer: Classroom lecture conductor.
  • CollegeAdmin: Institutional manager.
Cross-Ecosystem 6 Roles

Global participants with access across campus networks and talent pools:

  • HR: Corporate talent recruiter.
  • Speaker: Keynote guest speaker.
  • Sponsor: Innovation sprint funder.
  • CommunityLeader: Tech organizer.
  • MediaPartner: Press representative.
  • Member: General community user.
Platform Governance 2 Roles

Trusted internal operators. Cannot self-register (provisioned manually):

  • ForgeAdmin: Super administrator.
  • CourseAdmin: Curriculum author.

2 Granular Responsibilities & Seed Test Credentials

Every role has a dedicated persona, distinct navigation hierarchy, data permissions, and a pre-configured seed test account using the universal password: test1234.

1. Student (student) Cluster 1 & 2

Target Persona: College undergraduate builder developing technical and entrepreneurial competency.

  • Capabilities: Enrolls in courses, watches lessons, completes assignments, submits Citadel Sprint milestones, books 1-on-1 mentor sessions, and generates PDF certificates.
  • Data Scoping: Read-only access to enrolled courses; write access to own submissions and bookings.
  • Seed Fixtures: student1@forgeloom.dev (FIT) & student2@forgeloom.dev (Riverside).
2. Mentor (mentor) Cluster 1 & 2

Target Persona: Industry practitioner guiding students through innovation sprints.

  • Capabilities: Inspects assigned mentees, reviews Citadel team sprint deliverables, inputs milestone evaluations, and sets 1-on-1 availability slots.
  • Data Scoping: Scoped to assigned students and teams within their college.
  • Seed Fixtures: mentor1@forgeloom.dev (FIT) & mentor2@forgeloom.dev (Riverside).
3. Trainer (trainer) Cluster 1 & 2

Target Persona: Academic or technical educator delivering curriculum modules.

  • Capabilities: Schedules course batch sessions, logs student attendance, designs module assignments, grades student submissions, and sets module tests.
  • Data Scoping: Scoped to enrolled students in assigned course batches.
  • Seed Fixtures: trainer1@forgeloom.dev (FIT) & trainer2@forgeloom.dev (Riverside).
4. College Admin (college_admin) Cluster 1 & 2

Target Persona: University Dean, HOD, or Placement Director governing campus cohorts.

  • Capabilities: Views institution student roster, monitors faculty/trainer performance, oversees department completion rates, and tracks hiring analytics.
  • Data Scoping: Full institutional visibility across all users sharing their collegeId.
  • Seed Fixtures: college1@forgeloom.dev (FIT) & college2@forgeloom.dev (Riverside).
5. HR / Recruiter (hr) Cross-Ecosystem

Target Persona: Corporate hiring manager seeking pre-screened technical talent.

  • Capabilities: Maintains corporate profile, filters students in the Talent Pool by Builder Score (XP) and skill tags, inspects verified project portfolios, and shortlists candidates.
  • Data Scoping: Global read access to opted-in student builder profiles across all colleges.
  • Seed Fixture: hr1@forgeloom.dev.
6. Speaker (speaker) Cross-Ecosystem

Target Persona: Keynote industry speaker delivering masterclasses and guest tech talks.

  • Capabilities: Manages scheduled sessions, uploads presentation slides to S3, reviews audience feedback, proposes keynote topics, and connects with HR.
  • Data Scoping: Scoped to assigned speaking engagements and presentation assets.
  • Seed Fixture: speaker1@forgeloom.dev.

3 Roles 7 through 12 Breakdown

7. Sponsor (sponsor) Cross-Ecosystem

Target Persona: Corporate or venture partner sponsoring student innovation tracks.

  • Capabilities: Funds Citadel sprints, tracks funded project milestone submissions, reviews student demo day pitches, and gains branding across campus events.
  • Data Scoping: Scoped to sponsored events and sponsored challenge teams.
  • Seed Fixture: sponsor1@forgeloom.dev.
8. Community Leader (community_leader) Cross-Ecosystem

Target Persona: Campus lead or local tech community chapter organizer.

  • Capabilities: Organizes local tech meetups, coordinates student volunteers, facilitates peer discussions, and manages chapter outreach.
  • Data Scoping: Access to community chapter members and public discussion feeds.
  • Seed Fixture: community1@forgeloom.dev.
9. Media Partner (media_partner) Cross-Ecosystem

Target Persona: Journalist or media organization covering student innovation showcases.

  • Capabilities: Accesses official press releases, downloads brand media kits, and submits interview requests for top student innovators.
  • Data Scoping: Access to public media assets and approved student press kits.
  • Seed Fixture: media1@forgeloom.dev.
10. Member (member) Cross-Ecosystem

Target Persona: Tech enthusiast, self-paced learner, or alumnus.

  • Capabilities: Browses public course catalog, registers for open webinars, and participates in ecosystem community forums.
  • Data Scoping: Read access to public events and community discussions.
  • Seed Fixture: member1@forgeloom.dev.
11. Forge Admin (forge_admin) Super Admin

Target Persona: Executive platform director overseeing all colleges and global infrastructure.

  • Capabilities: Cross-institutional analytics, onboards new college tenants, audits global users, configures system settings, and inspects database health.
  • Data Scoping: Complete, unconstrained global read/write across all colleges.
  • Seed Fixture: admin1@forgeloom.dev (Manually seeded).
12. Course Admin (course_admin) Curriculum Author

Target Persona: Chief instructional designer creating certified learning tracks.

  • Capabilities: Authors courses, configures module and lesson hierarchies, embeds video lecture links, creates tests, and publishes to the catalog.
  • Data Scoping: Full control over the global course catalog and curriculum data.
  • Seed Fixture: course_admin1@forgeloom.dev (Manually seeded).
